    Stay safe when you apply
    Use the Report button on any listing that looks wrong.
    
    
    
      
    Never pay to get a job
    A real employer does not ask for registration, uniform or training fees before you start.
    
      
    Verified is a check, not a promise
    The hub checks employers before listing them, but cannot guarantee any job. Meet in public places and tell someone where you are going.
    
      
    Protect your documents
    Do not send your NRC, bank PIN or mobile money PIN with an application.
